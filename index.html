Privacy Policy – WATCH OUT!

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use WATCH OUT!, a mobile game developed and published by Mon Studio, operated by MOHAMED Issam Haimoura ("Mon Studio", "we", "us", or "our").

This policy applies specifically to WATCH OUT! and describes information stored or processed by the game and by third-party services integrated into it, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

2. Information Stored Locally

WATCH OUT! stores limited game information locally on your device to provide basic functionality, including your best score and other local game-state information required for operation.

This information is stored locally and is not transmitted by WATCH OUT! to Mon Studio servers.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

WATCH OUT! uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. In the current version, an interstitial advertisement may be shown after a defined number of game losses. Advertisements are not displayed during active gameplay.

According to Google's current documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address
  • User product interactions, including app launches, taps and ad or video interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Advertising identifiers, App Set ID and, where applicable, other device or account identifiers

The exact data processed may depend on Google Mobile Ads features and advertising settings. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

See Google Privacy Policy .

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

WATCH OUT! uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to manage consent requirements relating to advertising.

Depending on your location and applicable legal requirements, you may be shown a consent message and offered choices concerning personalized advertising or other advertising-related processing.

Advertising choices may also be affected by Android and Google controls.

5. No User Account

WATCH OUT! does not require an account, name, email address or personal profile to play.

Mon Studio does not operate a user account system for WATCH OUT!.

6. Information Not Directly Requested by WATCH OUT!

WATCH OUT! does not intentionally request your name, postal address, telephone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise location.

The core game does not require contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or precise device location.

Third-party advertising services may nevertheless process technical or device information as described above.

7. How Information Is Used

  • Provide and maintain game functionality
  • Store game progress and best score locally
  • Display and manage advertisements
  • Support advertising measurement, security and fraud prevention through third-party services
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

8. Sharing

Mon Studio does not sell your personal information.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads may be collected and shared by Google and relevant service providers or partners for advertising, measurement, security and fraud-prevention purposes, subject to applicable policies and privacy choices.

9. Retention and Deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you delete it through device or application controls, such as uninstalling the app or clearing its application data.

Mon Studio does not maintain a central WATCH OUT! user-account or game-score database.

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

10. Children's Privacy

WATCH OUT! is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

WATCH OUT! does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to Mon Studio.

If the intended audience, advertising configuration, features or applicable law changes, Mon Studio will review the applicable children's privacy requirements and update the app and this policy as appropriate.

11. International Processing

Third-party providers, including Google, may process information on servers in countries other than your country of residence, subject to their policies and applicable legal safeguards.

12. Security

Mon Studio takes reasonable measures to protect information handled through WATCH OUT!.

Data transmitted by Google's Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's documentation.

Local game information is protected by Android's security mechanisms.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent where applicable.

Because WATCH OUT! does not maintain user accounts or a central personal profile database, locally stored game data can generally be managed through device controls.

For information processed by Google, applicable rights may need to be exercised directly with Google.

14. Changes

We may update this policy when the game, advertising services, applicable laws or data practices change.

Updated versions will be published at the same public URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
