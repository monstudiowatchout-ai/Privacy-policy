Privacy Policy – Mon Studio

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications and games developed and published by Mon Studio, operated by MOHAMED Issam Haimoura ("Mon Studio", "we", "us" or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared and protected when you use our applications and games (collectively, the "Applications").

Individual Applications may provide additional privacy notices where their specific features or data practices require additional information.

2. Information We Collect

Our Applications may handle different categories of information depending on the features and services used by each Application.

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

Some Applications may store information locally on your device in order to provide their functionality.

Depending on the Application, this may include:

  • Game scores
  • Game progress
  • Gameplay preferences
  • Application settings

For example, WATCH OUT! currently stores the player's best score locally on the device.

Information stored locally by an Application is not transmitted to Mon Studio unless the relevant Application specifically states otherwise.

2.2 Information collected by third-party services

Some Applications may use third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, security or other technical services.

These third-party services may automatically process certain information from your device or your interaction with an Application.

For Applications using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), this may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Application and user interactions
  • Advertising-related information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information relating to advertisements displayed or interacted with

3. Advertising

Some Mon Studio Applications may display advertisements.

Where advertising is used, we may use third-party advertising providers, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

These providers may process information for purposes including:

  • Providing advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Analytics
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Improving advertising services

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share certain information, including information such as IP address, user interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, an Application may request your consent before certain data is processed for advertising or other purposes.

Mon Studio may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent and privacy choices where applicable.

The availability of consent choices may depend on your location and applicable law.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on the Application, Mon Studio may use third-party services to provide advertising, analytics, security, technical functionality or other services.

Examples may include:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Third-party service providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

6. How We Use Information

Depending on the Application and the services used, information may be processed for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating the Application
  • Saving game progress or preferences
  • Providing advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Understanding application performance
  • Detecting fraud or abuse
  • Maintaining security
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Improving our Applications and services
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

Mon Studio does not sell users' personal information.

7. Data Sharing

Mon Studio does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may nevertheless be processed or shared with third-party service providers where necessary to provide services used by an Application, including advertising, analytics, security or technical services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or valid governmental request.

8. Data Security

Mon Studio takes reasonable measures to protect information handled directly by its Applications.

Where information is transmitted by third-party services, those services may use security measures such as encryption in transit.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

The retention period depends on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

Information stored locally by an Application may remain on the device until it is deleted by the Application, application data is cleared, or the Application is uninstalled.

Mon Studio does not currently maintain a user account system for its Applications.

Information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their respective policies and retention practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Mon Studio Applications are generally intended for a broad audience unless a particular Application is specifically identified as being directed toward children.

Our Applications do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to Mon Studio.

If a particular Application is designed or directed toward children, additional privacy, advertising and safety requirements may apply and the relevant Application may provide additional information.
